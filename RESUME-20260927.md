## What changed in this stretch (all on hardware)

1. **VRAM fast path** (`31ff820`): VRAM writes go straight into the block
   RAM (`mem_vram_wp`) and are acknowledged the next clock; VRAM reads skip
   `wombat_bus32` and are acknowledged from the block RAM output.  Color
   13.97 -> 12.61 s.
2. **P245 MOVE16 chaining** (`0679ca8`): each longword transfer issues from
   the previous one's acknowledge.  Color 12.61 -> 11.42 s.
3. **ROM retained line** (`faf9d98`): every ROM read fetches its 16-byte line
   from DDR3 in one burst; the I-cache's other fill beats come from it.
   QuickDraw runs from ROM and misses the 8 KB I-cache.  Color 11.42 -> 9.87 s.
4. **Dani's CPU fix** (`6f0f159`, from danifunker/NeXT-Color_MiSTer
   `6d2e1a5`): a recorded prefetch fault no longer overwrites the in-flight
   data access's registers (endless fault loop, the NeXTSTEP `cc` hang; A/UX
   could hit the same).  Plus `--unroll-count 256` in `verilator/Makefile`:
   without it the MMU's 128-entry ATC clear (PFLUSHA) was dropped in the sim.

## Where the remaining gaps are (vs a real Quadra 800)

- **FPU 68 %**: 41 % of the old simulation's clocks are cache fills after Mac OS's own
  flushes -- 23.9k CPUSHA from **HLock** (ROM `$40887800` -> `jCacheFlush`
  `$6F4` -> `$40885032`) and 48.9k CPUSHL from a System patch at `$9AD6E`.
  The old ~15-clock simulated refill is not a hardware measurement: that
  model omitted the retained RAM-line sideband (see the correction above).
  Next ideas:
  faster line fills (safe, helps graphics too); or a CPUSHA that keeps the
  write-through, snooped data cache (requires architectural, all-writer and
  posted-write ordering review; walker U/M-bit snooping already exists).
- **Color 8-bit 83 %**, **Dhrystone 78 %**, **Disk 47 %** (Main's per-sector
  round trip).
