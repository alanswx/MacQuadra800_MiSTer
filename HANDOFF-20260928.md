Cache-v2 fills a clean first-line miss for a crossing read, suppresses operand
ACK until replay, and uses only an already-complete retained line for the
nonoperand tail. Missing sideband invalidates the partial victim and falls
back. It never adds external nonoperand tail reads while the operand waits.
The earlier `fb7e4e…` draft is rejected because its extra tail reads could
expose an inappropriate bus error. Do not build or revive that draft.

Native profiling established repeated first-line misses at physical `0x600eae`.
The candidate's aggregate bus counters improve, but its unchanged monitor does
not prove exact per-address event attribution. ROM latency6 is a fixture model;
there is no independent numerical oracle or new hardware speed measurement.
